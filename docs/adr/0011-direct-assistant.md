# ADR 0011: Sirius is a direct assistant, not a calming one

Status: accepted, 2026-11-03. Decided by the owner, who found the reassuring tone grating.

The original notes made "calm over productivity" binding, and the copy followed it: "No rush, it
will keep", "Rest is a good answer", "The time is yours", minute counts ending in "no more".

Decision: Sirius works for the person. It answers first, says what it did, and never tells them
to rest or slow down unless asked. The header's "What should I do now?" suggests only real work
from the dashboard. Alerts, errors and voice replies say what happened, and nothing more.

Kept: the visual calm, meaning the falling leaves, the breathing exercise and its nudges, and
the absence of scores, streaks and percentages.
