Strong simple silhouette, elegant geometric curves, balanced generous negative space, legible at 24px. Flat solid near-black ink, no gradients, no texture, no letters, no mockup. Transparent background. Center one symbol with ample padding. Inspired by wolf moon emblems but original, friendly alert intelligent rather than snarling.
